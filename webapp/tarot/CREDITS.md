# Tilia Tarot

An original deck made for Tilia (a private coaching bot), not a reproduction of any published deck.

Art direction: traditional occult tarot symbolism intertwined with linden (Tilia) botany. Antique engraved linework on warm ivory paper, forest green foliage, restrained antique gold, classic serif titles, and a consistent botanical border. Linden motifs include heart-shaped serrated leaves, pale flower clusters, and narrow floral bracts. The 78 cards share one back.

The masters were generated with an image tool and selected by the deck's owner. The files here are 600x900 WebP copies, rebuilt from the masters by `scripts/build_deck.py`.
